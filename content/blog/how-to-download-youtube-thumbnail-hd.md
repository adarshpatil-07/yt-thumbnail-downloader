## Method 2: Manual Browser URL Method (For Tech-Savvy Users)

Every YouTube video is assigned a unique 11-character Video ID. YouTube stores thumbnail files on its public image servers using predictable URL patterns.

If you know a video's 11-character ID (for example, `dQw4w9WgXcQ` from `youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ`), you can construct the direct image link manually in your browser address bar:

- **Maximum HD (1280x720):** `https://img.youtube.com/vi/VIDEO_ID/maxresdefault.jpg`
- **Standard HD (640x480):** `https://img.youtube.com/vi/VIDEO_ID/sddefault.jpg`
- **High Quality (480x360):** `https://img.youtube.com/vi/VIDEO_ID/hqdefault.jpg`

Once the image opens in your browser tab, right-click the image and select **Save image as...** to save it.
