# Privacy Policy

At **YT Thumbnail Downloader**, user privacy and transparency are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy details how our website functions, what data is processed, and how your privacy is protected when using our tool.

## Client-Side Processing (No Data Collection)

Our YouTube thumbnail downloader is engineered as a static, client-side web application. All URL parsing, thumbnail extraction, quality detection, and download operations take place directly within your device's web browser.

- **No Server Storage:** We do not host a database, backend server, or user tracking logs.
- **No User Input Logging:** Video URLs, video IDs, search queries, or downloaded files are never stored, tracked, or recorded by us.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to log in, create an account, or share personal contact information to use our service.

## Local Storage

We use your browser's `localStorage` solely to save your preferred visual theme setting (**Dark Mode** or **Light Mode**). This ensures that your display preference is remembered when you revisit the website. No personal or tracking data is stored in `localStorage`.

## Third-Party Network Requests

When you paste a YouTube video URL into the tool, your browser communicates directly with YouTube's public content distribution infrastructure to fetch thumbnail images and metadata:

1. **YouTube Image Servers (`img.youtube.com`):** Your browser requests thumbnail images directly from YouTube's public image servers (`https://img.youtube.com/vi/VIDEO_ID/...`).
2. **YouTube oEmbed API (`www.youtube.com/oembed`):** To display the video title and channel name above your thumbnail results, your browser makes a standard request to YouTube's public oEmbed metadata endpoint.

These direct requests are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not act as an intermediary proxy for these requests.

## Cookies and Analytics

YT Thumbnail Downloader does not set any tracking cookies, third-party advertising cookies, or analytics scripts.
