# About YT Thumbnail Downloader

Welcome to **YT Thumbnail Downloader**, a fast, free, and privacy-conscious online tool built for video creators, graphic designers, digital marketers, and media researchers.

## Our Purpose

YouTube thumbnails are key elements of video content design and online storytelling. Whether you are analyzing graphic design trends, saving cover artwork for inspiration, or retrieving your own video assets for archival purposes, access to high-quality thumbnail files should be simple and instant.

YT Thumbnail Downloader was created to provide a clean, ad-free, and distraction-free experience. You paste a video URL, select your desired resolution, and download the full-quality image file with a single click.

## Key Features

- **All Resolutions Supported:** Download cover images in Maximum HD (1280x720), Standard HD (640x480), High Quality (480x360), Medium Quality (320x180), and Default (120x90).
- **100% Client-Side Privacy:** The tool runs directly inside your web browser. No search history, URLs, or personal data are ever sent to or saved on a backend server.
- **Multiple Link Formats:** Supports standard YouTube watch URLs, `youtu.be` shortened links, Shorts videos, embedded players, and live streams.
- **Dark Mode Support:** Built with mobile-first design principles and automatic dark/light mode switching for comfortable viewing day or night.
